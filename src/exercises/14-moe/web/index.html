14 · Mixture of experts

One layer, copied into experts that then drift apart

A trained model is turned into a mixture of experts by copying one of its layers many times over. At the moment of conversion nothing changes — not a single output — and from there the copies are trained apart. Beside it, the unconverted model trains on exactly the same text, so what the experts bought can be told apart from what more training bought.
