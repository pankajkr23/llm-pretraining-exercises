Raw data is not training data

Eight named stages stand between the two. We ran all of them over three real corpora, counting tokens with our own tokenizer rather than estimating them — and watched most of the corpus disappear. The stages that cut hardest were not the ones we expected, and three of the nine quality rules turned out not to be language-neutral.
