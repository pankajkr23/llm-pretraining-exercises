07 · Model embeddings internals

Deleting the output head

Every language model carries a list with one row of numbers per word — and it carries that list twice, once to read words in and once to choose the next one. At a million words that is 768 million numbers per copy, memorised, doing nothing else. A published trick deletes the first copy by working each row out from the word's spelling, then says the second copy cannot follow and lists that under Limitations. It can. This page is the argument, with the measurements underneath it.
