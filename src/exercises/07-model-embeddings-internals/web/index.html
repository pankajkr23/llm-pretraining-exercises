07 · Model embeddings internals

Deleting the output head

A language model keeps one row of numbers per word — and it keeps that table twice, once to read words in and once to choose the next one. At a million words that is 768 million parameters per door. Kronecker embeddings delete the first table by computing each row from the word's own spelling. Their paper then says the second door cannot follow, and lists it under Limitations. It can. The code inverts exactly, the head ties to it, and the result beats the paper's own design on 5 of 5 seeds with fewer parameters.
