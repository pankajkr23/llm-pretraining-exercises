12 · Distributed training

Every weight costs the same bytes; ZeRO chooses where they live

Training a model keeps a fixed bill of bytes for every weight it learns. Plain data parallelism hands the whole bill to every device. ZeRO keeps the same bill and splits it across the devices, one kind of state at a time. Here a simulated cluster trains one small model each way, and every byte a device keeps or sends is counted against arithmetic done by hand.
