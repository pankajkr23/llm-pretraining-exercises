Out of what?

A language model becomes what it reads. Deciding how much of each kind of text it reads looks like choosing seven percentages that add to 100 — and anyone can do that. The work is answering one question for each of them: out of what? Do it honestly and some of those numbers stop being affordable: one lane here asks for more than any amount of re-reading could ever be worth, and another turns out to be counting the same text twice.
