10 · The training loop

A loop that tells the truth about itself

A training loop will not tell you it is wrong. It will show you a loss going down. So this one is made to report on itself six ways, and not one of them rewards a low loss — each is a measurement of the loop, or a deliberate breakage of it. Two of the numbers it first produced were flattering themselves, and a third was a bug in code that passed its own test.
